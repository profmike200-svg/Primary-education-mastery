# Learning situation: "Our Town Guide for Visitors" (6º de Primaria, Inglés)

> Ejemplo ficticio redactado como modelo para el aula y para las oposiciones (Castilla-La Mancha). Completar los códigos oficiales de competencias específicas y criterios de evaluación con el Decreto 81/2022. Sin datos de alumnos reales.

## 1. Identification
- **Year**: 6º (third cycle) · **Timing**: 8 sessions of 60 minutes.
- **Link with other areas**: Conocimiento del Medio (local heritage), Competencia digital.
- **Challenge**: *Our town will receive visitors from a partner school abroad. In teams, students create an illustrated bilingual guide (printed and digital) with places to visit, directions and recommendations, and guide the visitors on a short video call.*

## 2. Curricular references
- **Key competences**: CCL, CP, CD, CC, CE, CCEC.
- **Specific competences**: comprehension (written: real tourist leaflets), production (written guide, oral video), interaction (video call), **mediation** (explaining local information to foreign visitors), plurilingualism and interculturality.
- **Basic knowledge**: places in town, directions (*go straight on, turn left, next to, opposite*), *there is / there are*, *can* for recommendations, *should*, superlatives (*the oldest, the best*); text type: guide/leaflet.

## 3. Learning objectives
1. Understand authentic tourist texts with support.
2. Give and follow simple directions orally.
3. Write a short guide entry (60-80 words) following a model and a checklist.
4. Mediate: explain information about the town to visitors in simple English.

| Session | Activities | Evidence base |

| 8 | Video call with the visitors (or simulated); reflection *I used to think… now I think…*; retrieval quiz | [[Practica_de_Recuperacion]] |

## 5. Inclusion (Decreto 85/2018)
- UDL: model texts, speaking frames, glossary with images, choice of format (written page, audio guide, video).
- [[Dislexia]]: audio guide option; spelling not penalised; text-to-speech.
- [[TDAH]]: roles with movement (map master, timekeeper); short checkpoints.
- [[TEA]]: clear roles and script for the video call.
- [[Altas_Capacidades]]: longer entries, interview a local expert, compare with a town in an English-speaking country.

## 6. Assessment
| Criterion | Instrument |
|---|---|
| Written comprehension | Jigsaw comprehension sheet |
| Written production | Analytic rubric shared in advance (content, organisation, vocabulary, structures, accuracy) |
| Oral production and interaction | Observation scale during rehearsal and video call |
| Mediation | Observation in the jigsaw and during the call |
| Self- and peer assessment | *Can-do* checklist |
