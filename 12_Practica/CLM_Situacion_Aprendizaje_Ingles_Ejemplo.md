# Learning situation: "Our Healthy Lunchbox" (4º de Primaria, Inglés)

> Ejemplo ficticio redactado como modelo para el aula y para las oposiciones. Verificar los códigos de las competencias específicas y los criterios de evaluación con el Decreto 81/2022 de Castilla-La Mancha antes de usarlo. Sin datos de alumnos reales.

## 1. Identification
- **Year**: 4º (second cycle) · **Timing**: 6 sessions of 60 minutes (2 weeks, third term).
- **Linked areas**: Conocimiento del Medio (healthy habits), Educación Física.
- **Challenge**: *The school canteen wants ideas for healthy break-time snacks. Each team will design a healthy lunchbox and present it in English in a short video for the school website.*

## 2. Curricular references
- **Key competences**: CCL, CP, STEM, CD, CPSAA, CC.
- **Specific competences of Foreign Language**: comprehension (oral and written texts), production, interaction, mediation (explaining a recipe card to a classmate), interculturality (lunchboxes in English-speaking countries).
- **Basic knowledge**: food and quantities vocabulary; *there is / there are; some / any; I like / I don't like; It's healthy because…*; phonics /ʃ/ vs /tʃ/ (*fish, cheese, chips, sandwich*); healthy lifestyle.
- **Assessment criteria**: those of the second cycle for the competences above (insert the official codes from Decreto 81/2022).

## 3. Learning objectives (observable)
By the end, students will be able to:
1. Understand a short oral and written text about food and healthy habits.
2. Use at least 12 food words and the structures *there is / there are, some / any*.
3. Describe and justify a healthy lunchbox in a 1-minute oral presentation.
4. Interact to ask and answer about food preferences.

## 4. Sequence of activities
| Session | Phase | Activities | Evidence base |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Activation and input | Mystery bag with real food; prior knowledge (*What do you eat at break time?*); pre-teach 12 words with flashcards and gestures; story *Oliver's Vegetables* or similar read aloud | [[Conocimiento_Previo]] · [[Rutina_Cuentacuentos_en_Ingles]] |
| 2 | Explicit focus on form | Model *there is / there are, some / any* with a real lunchbox; guided practice with mini-whiteboards; phonics /ʃ/ vs /tʃ/ | [[Instruccion_Explicita]] · [[Ensenanza_Comunicativa_con_Foco_en_la_Forma]] |
| 3 | Interaction | Class survey *Do you like…?* in pairs; bar chart of results (link to maths) | [[Estadistica_y_Probabilidad]] |
| 4 | Reading and mediation | Read healthy recipe cards in teams (jigsaw); each expert explains their card to the team | [[Aprendizaje_Cooperativo]] |
| 5 | Production | Teams design their lunchbox (drawing + labels) and rehearse the presentation with a speaking frame; peer feedback with the rubric | [[Rubricas]] |
| 6 | Final product and reflection | Record the 1-minute videos; whole-class viewing; *I used to think… now I think…*; retrieval quiz of the vocabulary | [[Rutinas_de_Pensamiento]] · [[Practica_de_Recuperacion]] |

Spaced review of the vocabulary in later units ([[Practica_Espaciada]]).

## 5. Inclusion (Decreto 85/2018, [[CLM_Inclusion_Educativa]])
- **UDL for all**: visuals, real objects, gestures, speaking frames, choice in the final product (video, poster with audio, live presentation) ([[Diseno_Universal_para_el_Aprendizaje]]).
- [[TDAH]]: short tasks with movement (survey around the class), role of "time keeper".
- [[Dislexia]]: oral focus, picture dictionary, reading in pairs, no penalty for spelling in the final product.
- [[TEA]]: visual schedule of the 6 sessions, clear role in the team, warning before the recording.
- [[Incorporacion_Tardia]]: picture dictionary, peer buddy, use of the home language to activate knowledge.
- [[Altas_Capacidades]]: compare lunchboxes in two English-speaking countries; write a short recipe.

## 6. Assessment
| Criterion | Instrument | Moment |
|---|---|---|
| Oral comprehension | Listening task with pictures (session 1 and 6) | Initial / final |
| Use of vocabulary and structures | Mini-whiteboards, exit tickets, retrieval quiz | Formative, every session |
| Oral production | Analytic rubric (content, vocabulary, structures, pronunciation, fluency), shared beforehand | Final product |
| Interaction | Observation scale during the survey | Session 3 |
| Self- and peer assessment | Child-friendly checklist with icons | Sessions 5-6 |

Oral feedback with **prompts** for self-correction ([[Lyster_2010_Feedback_Oral_L2]]). Teacher self-evaluation of the learning situation at the end.
