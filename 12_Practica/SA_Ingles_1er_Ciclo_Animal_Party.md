# Learning situation: "The Animal Party" (2º de Primaria, Inglés)

> Ejemplo ficticio redactado como modelo para el aula y para las oposiciones (Castilla-La Mancha). Completar los códigos oficiales de competencias específicas y criterios de evaluación con el Decreto 81/2022. Sin datos de alumnos reales.

## 1. Identification
- **Year**: 2º (first cycle) · **Timing**: 6 sessions of 45 minutes.
- **Challenge**: *The animals of the zoo are having a party! Each child creates an animal mask and presents their animal to the class: what it looks like, what it can do and what it likes eating. Final event: the Animal Party with a song and a parade for 1º.*

## 2. Curricular references
- **Key competences**: CCL, CP, CPSAA, CCEC.
- **Specific competences**: comprehension (oral), production (oral), interaction; interculturality (animals from English-speaking countries: kangaroo, koala, moose).
- **Basic knowledge**: animals (10-12 words), colours, body parts; *It's a… It's big/small. It can… It likes…*; songs and chants; rhythm.

## 3. Learning objectives
1. Understand a short story and a song about animals.
2. Name 10 animals and 4 actions (*jump, swim, fly, run*).
3. Say 3 sentences about an animal using *It's… It can… It likes…*.

## 4. Sequence
| Session | Activities | Evidence base |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Picture book about animals read with gestures and voices; flashcards; TPR (*jump like a kangaroo!*) | [[Respuesta_Fisica_Total_TPR]] · [[Rutina_Cuentacuentos_en_Ingles]] |
| 2 | Song with actions; game *Guess the animal* (mime); structure *It can…* modelled | [[Instruccion_Explicita]] |
| 3 | Re-reading the story, children complete the repeated phrases; pair game with cards (*Can it swim? Yes, it can*) | [[Expresion_e_Interaccion_Oral_L2]] |
| 4 | Make the mask; label with key words (copying from model) | [[Lectura_y_Escritura_L2]] |
| 5 | Rehearse the 3-sentence presentation with a speaking frame, first in pairs, then in small groups | [[Andamiaje]] |
| 6 | Animal Party: parade, song and presentations to 1º; retrieval game of all the words | [[Practica_de_Recuperacion]] |

## 5. Inclusion (Decreto 85/2018)
- Visual and gestural support for all; choice to present alone, in pairs or with a puppet.
- [[TDAH]]: movement in every session; role of "zoo keeper".
- [[TEA]]: visual schedule; warning before the party; quiet place if the noise is too much.
- [[Incorporacion_Tardia]]: buddy; animal names in their home language on the class word wall.
- [[Altas_Capacidades]]: describe two animals and compare them (*bigger than*).

## 6. Assessment
- Observation checklist with icons: understands the story, names animals, uses *It can…*.
- Self-assessment with faces (😀 🙂 😐) for "I can name animals / I can say what animals can do".
