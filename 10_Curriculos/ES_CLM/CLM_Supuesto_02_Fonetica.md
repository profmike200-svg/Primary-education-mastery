# Supuesto 2: Fonética y pronunciación

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
1. Transcribe the following words and sentence using the IPA (British English, standard pronunciation): *ship, sheep, thought, school, walked, wanted, played, vegetables*. Sentence: *She wanted to buy some cheap shoes.*
2. Identify which features of these words and of the sentence are usually difficult for Spanish-speaking pupils, and explain why.
3. Propose three activities to work on these difficulties with 5th-year pupils.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1. Transcription (British English, citation forms).**
| Word | IPA |
|---|---|
| ship | /ʃɪp/ |
| sheep | /ʃiːp/ |
| thought | /θɔːt/ |
| school | /skuːl/ |
| walked | /wɔːkt/ |
| wanted | /ˈwɒntɪd/ |
| played | /pleɪd/ |
| vegetables | /ˈvedʒtəbəlz/ |

Sentence (connected speech, weak forms): /ʃi ˈwɒntɪd tə ˈbaɪ səm ˈtʃiːp ˈʃuːz/

*Check transcriptions with a pronunciation dictionary (e.g. Cambridge or Longman) when practising.*

**2. Difficulties for Spanish speakers.**
- **Vowel length and quality**: /ɪ/–/iː/ (*ship/sheep*) are not distinguished in Spanish, which has five vowels.
- **/θ/**: exists in most of Spain, so it is usually easy here, but /ð/ and the vowel /ɔː/ are new.
- **/ʃ/ and /tʃ/** (*she, shoes, cheap*): Spanish has /tʃ/ but not /ʃ/; pupils tend to say *chip* for *ship*.
- **Initial /s/ + consonant** (*school*): Spanish phonotactics add a vowel: *eschool*.
- **Past tense -ed**: three pronunciations (/t/ *walked*, /d/ *played*, /ɪd/ *wanted*); pupils pronounce it as an extra syllable: *walk-ed*.
- **Final consonant clusters and /z/** (*vegetables, shoes*): Spanish has few final consonants and no /z/ phoneme.
- **Weak forms and schwa** (*to* /tə/, *some* /səm/): Spanish has no vowel reduction; pupils give every syllable the same weight.
- **Word stress and rhythm**: English is stress-timed and Spanish syllable-timed; *vegetables* has 3 syllables, not 4.

**3. Activities for 5th year.**
1. *Minimal pairs bingo / stand-up-sit-down*: the teacher says *ship* or *sheep*; pupils choose the picture or move. Then pupils lead.
2. *-ed detectives*: sort past forms into three boxes (/t/, /d/, /ɪd/) by listening; discover the rule (voiceless / voiced / after t-d); then a *chant* with the verbs.
3. *Rhythm clapping and jazz chants*: clap the stressed syllables in sentences (*She WANted to BUY some CHEAP SHOES*), practise weak forms, record and listen.
Principle: the aim is **intelligibility**, not a native accent; explicit pronunciation teaching is effective.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Exactitud de la transcripción (símbolos, acento, formas débiles).
- Análisis contrastivo **justificado** con el sistema fonológico del español.
- Actividades adecuadas a la edad, lúdicas y con base en la investigación.
