# Supuesto 7: Conflicto en el aula y tutoría

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
In your 6th-year English class, two pupils regularly laugh at a classmate's pronunciation when she speaks in English. The classmate has stopped participating and her family has told you she does not want to come to English lessons.

1. Analyse the situation.
2. Explain how you would act in the short and medium term, with reference to regulations and to the school's documents.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1. Analysis.** This situation affects **coexistence** and the pupil's **emotional well-being**; it may be the beginning of bullying (repetition, imbalance of power) and must not be minimised. It also creates **anxiety** about speaking, which blocks language learning.

**2. Action.**
*Short term:*
- Talk privately with the affected pupil: listen, reassure, agree on support.
- Talk separately with the two pupils: describe the behaviour, its impact, the class rules; restorative approach (repair the harm).
- Inform the tutor and, if there are signs of bullying, the head of studies and the school's **coordinator of well-being and protection**, and follow the protocol (LOPIVI; regional coexistence regulations; the school's NCOF and coexistence plan).
- Inform and listen to the family; agree on a plan and follow-up.

*Medium term (whole class):*
- Build a **safe classroom climate** for speaking: explicit norms (*We respect everyone's English*), the idea that errors are part of learning.
- Reduce exposure: pair and small-group speaking before whole-class; recordings; choice of format.
- Cooperative learning with roles; positive feedback focused on communication.
- Social-emotional activities in English (feelings vocabulary, empathy stories).
- Monitor and evaluate the measures.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Detección del posible acoso y activación del protocolo.
- Referencia a la normativa (LOPIVI, convivencia de Castilla-La Mancha, documentos del centro).
- Medidas preventivas de clima de aula y de reducción de la ansiedad al hablar.
- Colaboración con la familia y el equipo docente.
