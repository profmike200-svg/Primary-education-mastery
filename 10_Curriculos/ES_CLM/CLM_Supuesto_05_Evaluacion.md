# Supuesto 5: Diseño de la evaluación de una unidad

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
You are planning a learning situation for 6th year whose final task is an oral presentation about a famous person from an English-speaking country.

1. Design the assessment of this learning situation: criteria, instruments, moments and agents.
2. Design an analytic rubric for the oral presentation.
3. Explain how you would adapt the assessment for a pupil with a developmental language disorder.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1. Assessment design.** Referents: assessment criteria of the third cycle (Decreto 81/2022) for oral production, comprehension and interculturality; Orden 185/2022 (continuous, formative, global assessment).
| Moment | Instrument | Agent |
|---|---|---|
| Initial | Short quiz and KWL chart about famous people | Teacher; self |
| During | Exit tickets; observation of rehearsals; checklist of the research notes | Teacher; peers |
| Final | Analytic rubric of the presentation; *can-do* self-assessment | Teacher; self; peers |
| After | Teacher's evaluation of the learning situation | Teacher |

**2. Analytic rubric (4 levels).**
| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Content | Includes all the required information (who, where, when, why famous) and an interesting fact | All required information | Some information missing | Very little information |
| Organisation | Clear introduction, body and conclusion | Clear order | Some order | No clear order |
| Language | Uses past simple and vocabulary of the unit accurately | Few errors that do not affect meaning | Errors sometimes affect meaning | Errors often prevent understanding |
| Pronunciation and fluency | Intelligible, few pauses | Intelligible with some pauses | Sometimes difficult to understand | Very difficult to understand |
| Interaction with the audience | Eye contact, answers questions | Answers questions | Reads most of the time | Reads all the time |
Share it with pupils **before** the task and use it for self- and peer assessment.

**3. Adaptation for a pupil with DLD.** Keep the same criteria but adapt **access and format**: visual support (slides with pictures and key words), a speaking frame, extra preparation time, presenting to a small group or recording at home, questions announced in advance; focus feedback on communication. Coordination with the speech and language teacher (AL).

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Coherencia criterios-instrumentos-momentos-agentes.
- Rúbrica con descriptores claros y observables.
- Adaptación de **acceso** sin rebajar los criterios (salvo adaptación significativa).
