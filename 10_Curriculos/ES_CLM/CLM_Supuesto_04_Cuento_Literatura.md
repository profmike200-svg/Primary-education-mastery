# Supuesto 4: Explotación didáctica de un cuento

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
You want to use a well-known picture book with a repetitive structure (for example *Brown Bear, Brown Bear, What Do You See?* or *The Very Hungry Caterpillar*) with a 1st-year group.

1. Justify the use of stories in the English classroom.
2. Design a sequence of activities (before, while and after reading) over several sessions.
3. Explain how the story contributes to the key competences and to other areas.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1. Justification.** Stories provide meaningful, contextualised and repeated **input**; repetitive structures allow early production through chunks; pictures support comprehension; they engage emotion and imagination; they connect with literary education and reading for pleasure (LOMLOE gives priority to reading). Authors: Cameron (discourse and narrative in the young learner classroom), Ellis and Brewster (storytelling handbook).

**2. Sequence.**
- **Pre-reading**: predict from the cover; pre-teach key vocabulary with flashcards and gestures (colours, animals / food, days of the week); a song.
- **While reading**: read aloud with expression, voices and pauses; pupils join in with the repeated structure, point to pictures, do actions (TPR).
- **Post-reading** (following sessions): re-read (repetition is key); sequencing pictures; dramatisation with masks; *class book* with a new page each (*What do you see?* / *On Monday he ate…*); retrieval games; take the story home (family reading).

**3. Competences and interdisciplinarity.** CCL and CP (comprehension, production, plurilingualism: comparing with the Spanish version); STEM (*The Very Hungry Caterpillar*: life cycle, days of the week, counting); CCEC (illustration, art activity with collage in Eric Carle's style); CPSAA (cooperation in the dramatisation). Links with Conocimiento del Medio and Educación Artística.

**Assessment**: observation checklist (understands, joins in, uses the structure), the class book page.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Fundamentación teórica (input, repetición, motivación, autores).
- Secuencia antes, durante y después de la lectura completa y realista.
- Conexión con competencias clave y otras áreas.
