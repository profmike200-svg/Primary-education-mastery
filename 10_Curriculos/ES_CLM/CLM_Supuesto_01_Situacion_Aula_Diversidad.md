# Supuesto 1: Clase de 3º con diversidad

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
You are the English teacher of a 3rd-year group (22 pupils) in a state school in Castilla-La Mancha that is not part of a bilingual programme. In the group there is a pupil with ADHD, a pupil with dyslexia, a pupil who arrived from Morocco two months ago and speaks Arabic and a little Spanish, and two pupils who already have a high level of English. The next unit is about **daily routines**.

1. Design a sequence of activities (4 sessions) for this unit.
2. Explain the inclusion measures you would apply, with reference to current regulations.
3. Explain how you would assess the pupils' learning.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**Introduction.** Justify the answer with the current framework: LOMLOE, Royal Decree 157/2022, Decreto 81/2022 (curriculum) and Orden 185/2022 (assessment) of Castilla-La Mancha, and Decreto 85/2018 on inclusive education, which requires removing barriers to learning and participation for **all** pupils. Methodology: communicative approach with focus on form, Universal Design for Learning (UDL), cooperative learning.

**1. Sequence (4 sessions of 45-60 minutes).**
| Session | Activities | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: video or picture story *A day in the life of…*; prior knowledge with flashcards; TPR with routine actions (*get up, brush my teeth, have breakfast…*) | Comprehension; vocabulary input |
| 2 | Explicit focus on form: *I get up at 7 o'clock* / *What time do you…?*; telling the time (o'clock, half past); guided practice with mini-whiteboards | Structures; guided practice |
| 3 | Information-gap in pairs: find out a classmate's routine and complete a timetable; class survey and bar chart | Interaction; mediation |
| 4 | Final task: *My daily routine* comic or short video (4-6 sentences) with a speaking frame; presentation in small groups; retrieval game | Production; review |

**2. Inclusion measures (Decreto 85/2018).**
- *Classroom-level measures*: UDL (visuals, gestures, real objects, choice of format in the final task), cooperative structures with roles, peer tutoring.
- *Pupil with ADHD*: short tasks with movement (TPR, survey), visual agenda of the session, instructions one by one, immediate positive reinforcement, a role (time keeper).
- *Pupil with dyslexia*: priority to oral work, picture dictionary, reading in pairs, final product as a video or oral presentation, no penalty for spelling.
- *Newly arrived pupil*: peer buddy, visual support, key words also in Arabic/Spanish on the word wall, allow answers with gestures or drawings; coordination with the school's support and guidance team.
- *Pupils with a high level*: extension tasks (compare routines in two countries, write a longer text, use *always/usually/sometimes*); they should not be used only as helpers.

**3. Assessment.** Formative and continuous (Orden 185/2022), linked to the assessment criteria of the second cycle: observation scale during the pair work (interaction), exit tickets and mini-whiteboards (structures), analytic rubric shared in advance for the final task (production), *can-do* self-assessment with icons. Adapted formats for the pupils above without changing the criteria. Assessment of the teaching practice at the end of the unit.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Referencia correcta y **aplicada** a la normativa (no solo citarla).
- Coherencia entre objetivos, actividades y evaluación.
- Medidas de inclusión **concretas** para cada perfil, no genéricas.
- Metodología comunicativa justificada.
- Uso correcto del inglés (la prueba es en inglés).
