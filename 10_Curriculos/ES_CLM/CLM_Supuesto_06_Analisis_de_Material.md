# Supuesto 6: Análisis y mejora de una unidad del libro de texto

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
The unit of the 4th-year textbook you must use consists of: a vocabulary list of 15 words about clothes, a grammar box with *present continuous*, a reading text with comprehension questions, a fill-in-the-gaps exercise and a short dialogue to repeat in pairs.

1. Analyse the strengths and weaknesses of this unit from a communicative and competence-based perspective.
2. Propose how to transform it into a learning situation.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1. Analysis.**
- *Strengths*: clear vocabulary and grammar focus; some reading; a dialogue that gives an oral model.
- *Weaknesses*: decontextualised (no real purpose or final task); predominance of controlled, written practice; little meaningful interaction (repeating is not communicating); no listening input of quality; no attention to diversity; assessment probably limited to a test; vocabulary not selected by frequency or recycled.

**2. Transformation into a learning situation: *"Our Class Fashion Show"*.**
- Challenge: organise a fashion show for another class describing what models are wearing.
- Sequence: input with a video/song and real clothes (TPR); explicit focus on *present continuous* (*She's wearing…*) with pictures; information-gap (*Find the differences* between two pictures); reading of a short magazine text with a purpose (choose an outfit for a party); writing captions for photos; rehearsal and fashion show with commentators; spaced recycling of vocabulary in later units.
- Inclusion: roles (model, commentator, photographer, presenter), visual supports, choice.
- Assessment: observation scale, rubric for the commentary, self-assessment.
The textbook remains a **resource**, not the programme.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Análisis crítico con criterios explícitos (enfoque comunicativo, competencial, diversidad, evaluación).
- Propuesta realista que conserva lo útil del material.
