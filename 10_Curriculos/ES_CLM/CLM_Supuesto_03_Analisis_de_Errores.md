# Supuesto 3: Análisis de errores de un texto de alumno

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
A 5th-year pupil has written the following text about the weekend:

> *Yesterday I go to the park with my friends. We play football and I win. My brother don't like football, he prefer the basketball. In the park there is many trees. I am very happy because the day was sunny.*

1. Identify and classify the errors.
2. Explain their possible causes.
3. Explain how you would give feedback and which activities you would propose.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1–2. Errors, classification and causes.**
| Error | Correct form | Type | Probable cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| *I go, We play, I win* (yesterday) | *went, played, won* | Grammar: past simple | **Intralingual**: incomplete learning of the past; overuse of the base form |
| *My brother don't like* | *doesn't like* | Grammar: 3rd person auxiliary | Intralingual: overgeneralisation of *don't* |
| *he prefer* | *he prefers* | Grammar: 3rd person -s | Intralingual: the -s is a late-acquired, low-salience morpheme |
| *the basketball* | *basketball* | Article | **Interlingual (transfer)**: Spanish uses the article (*el baloncesto*) |
| *there is many trees* | *there are* | Agreement | Intralingual: *there is* learnt as a fixed chunk |
| *I am very happy* (yesterday) | *I was* | Tense consistency | Intralingual |

Concepts: errors are a natural sign of the learner's **interlanguage** (Selinker) and give information about the learning process (Corder). Distinguish *errors* (systematic) from *mistakes* (slips).

**3. Feedback and activities.**
- Prioritise: here the **past simple** is the main pattern; do not correct everything at once.
- Written feedback with codes (T = tense, A = agreement…) so that the pupil **self-corrects**; one specific comment on content (*Great description of your day!*) and one target for next time.
- Oral feedback with prompts (*Yesterday I… go?*) rather than only recasts.
- Activities: *weekend news* routine every Monday (oral, then written); sorting regular/irregular past verbs; a *spot the mistake* game with anonymous sentences; rewriting the text with a checklist; spaced retrieval of irregular verbs.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Clasificación rigurosa (gramatical, léxica, ortográfica; interlingüe e intralingüe).
- Conocimiento de la teoría (interlengua, errores frente a faltas).
- Retroalimentación formativa y priorizada; actividades de seguimiento.
