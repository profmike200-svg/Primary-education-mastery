# Supuesto 8: Enseñanza de una unidad de ciencias en inglés (AICLE)

> Supuesto de práctica redactado como modelo (no es un examen oficial). Contrastar el formato con los criterios de la parte B publicados en la convocatoria vigente. Datos de alumnos ficticios.

## Task (enunciado)
Your school has a bilingual programme and you teach Natural Sciences in English in 4th year. The next unit is **the water cycle**. Several pupils have difficulty understanding the content in English.

1. Plan the unit following CLIL principles.
2. Explain the language support (scaffolding) you would use.
3. Explain how you would assess content and language.

## Model answer (guía de resolución)
**1. Planning (4Cs framework, Coyle).**
- *Content*: states of water, evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection.
- *Communication*: language **of** learning (*evaporation, condensation*), **for** learning (*First…, then…, finally…; It changes from… to…*), **through** learning (new language emerging from the experiments).
- *Cognition*: describe, sequence, explain cause and effect, predict.
- *Culture*: water as a resource; sustainability in Castilla-La Mancha (droughts, rivers).
Sequence: hook (experiment: evaporation in a sunny window), explicit teaching with a labelled diagram, guided inquiry (model of the water cycle in a bag), reading a short adapted text, final product: an illustrated explanation or a short video.

**2. Scaffolding.** Pre-teach key vocabulary with images and gestures; visual organisers (cycle diagram with sentence starters); speaking frames; bilingual glossary; demonstrations; checking understanding of **content** frequently; strategic and limited use of Spanish for complex concepts; cooperative groups with roles.

**3. Assessment.** Separate criteria for content (science criteria) and language (but do not penalise science knowledge for language errors); visual or oral options to demonstrate understanding (labelling a diagram, sequencing pictures); rubric for the final product.
Note: the evidence on CLIL is positive for language but less clear for content learning; scaffolding is essential so that no pupil loses content.

## Qué suele valorar el tribunal en este tipo de supuesto
- Dominio del marco AICLE (4Cs, tipos de lenguaje).
- Andamiaje lingüístico y cognitivo concreto.
- Evaluación diferenciada de contenido y lengua.
- Conocimiento de la normativa de programas bilingües.
